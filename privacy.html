Privacy Policy — Runway Control

Package ID: com.runway_control • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Runway Control ("the App") is developed and published by Karthik Mungamuru. Your privacy matters. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information. The App operates completely offline and does not require account registration, sign-in, location access, camera/microphone access, or contacts access.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your game progress—including unlocked stages, star ratings, high scores, in-game ATC Credits, Tower Upgrades, Daily Ops records, and sound/haptics preferences—is saved exclusively on your own device using Android local storage (SharedPreferences / localStorage). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared anytime by clearing app storage or uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App does not include third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, crash telemetry trackers, or social media tracking scripts.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data of any kind, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with COPPA and GDPR-K principles.

5. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional online features (such as cloud leaderboards), this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are enabled.
